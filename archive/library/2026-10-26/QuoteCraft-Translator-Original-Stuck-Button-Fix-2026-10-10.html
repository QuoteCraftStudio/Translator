About audio & privacy

Open this link in Chrome or Safari rather than inside Facebook. Microphone access and an internet connection are required. Speech recognition may send audio to your browser’s speech provider. Recognized words are sent to MyMemory for translation; its free daily allowance is limited. Quote Craft does not save conversations. Language choices are remembered on this device. Voices and available languages depend on your device and browser. Translated words remain on screen until the next sentence for that side or a language change, and are not saved. Keep sentences short. Translations can contain mistakes.
